I am an interventional cardiologist at Einstein Hospital Israelita in São Paulo, Brazil, where I have worked since 2021. I am a direct PhD candidate at the Albert Einstein Faculty of Health Sciences (FICSAE) and a PhD candidate in the DigiCardioPath program at Sapienza University of Rome.

On this site I publish critical reviews of scientific papers and posts on statistics, clinical research and computing.

## Training

- MD, Botucatu Medical School (UNESP), 2011.
- Residency in internal medicine (2013–2015), cardiology (2015–2017) and interventional cardiology and hemodynamics (2017–2019) at the Heart Institute (InCor), University of São Paulo.
- Board certified in cardiology (SBC, 2017) and in interventional cardiology (SBHCI/AMB, 2019). TAVI certification (SBHCI/SBCCV/AMB).

## Research

- Intravascular imaging (IVUS and OCT) and plaque inflammatory phenotype.
- Coronary physiology (FFR, iFR, QFR, UFR).
- Pre-TAVI computed tomography and coronary assessment.
- Calcium modification: intravascular lithotripsy and rotational atherectomy.
- Drug-coated balloons.
- Computational modeling of coronary geometry and blood flow.

I have 19 publications from 2018 to 2026, including papers in the *Journal of the American College of Cardiology*, *EuroIntervention* and *Coronary Artery Disease*. I have presented at EuroPCR every year from 2022 to 2026. Awards: best abstract at the SBHCI Congress 2025 (FIRE-OCT study) and at EuroPCR RioValves 2025 (TACT study).

## Statistics and computing

- Applied biostatistics: survival analysis, competing risks, logistic regression, propensity-score matching, Bland-Altman analysis and intraclass correlation.
- Analysis in R with reproducible pipelines, from the analysis plan to the manuscript tables.
- Python for data auditing, exploratory analysis and machine learning.
- In development: automatic IVUS segmentation software and a deep-learning model to detect severe aortic stenosis from facial video.

## Peer review

Reviewer for EuroIntervention, European Heart Journal, BMC journals and Arquivos Brasileiros de Cardiologia.
