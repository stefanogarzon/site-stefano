title: 'NODULE-SHOCK: rotational atherectomy before lithotripsy for calcified nodules'
date: 2026-11-14
lang: en
description: Atherectomy before IVL enlarged minimum stent area at the calcified nodule, but part of the gain may come from larger stents and vessels, and the endpoint is imaging.
reference: Kini AS, Yasumura K, Nakao S, Oliva A, Revaiah PC, Vengrenyuk Y, et al. Rotational Atherectomy Plus Intravascular Lithotripsy Versus Intravascular Lithotripsy Alone for Coronary Calcified Nodules. The NODULE-SHOCK Trial. JACC Cardiovasc Interv. 2026 (published online).
doi: 10.1016/j.jcin.2026.10.001
design: Single-center randomized trial, two OCT-defined cohorts (NCT07000045)
'n': 120
summary: |-
  NODULE-SHOCK randomized 1:1 within two OCT-defined cohorts. In cohort A, with a calcified nodule (n = 66), rotational atherectomy (RA) followed by IVL versus IVL alone. In cohort B, severe calcium without a nodule (n = 54), maximum IVL pulses versus operator-determined pulses. Everyone had OCT before, after calcium modification and after stenting.

  | Cohort | Minimum stent area | Difference (95% CI) |
  |---|---|---|
  | A: RA + IVL vs IVL, at the nodule | 7.41 ± 2.12 vs 6.23 ± 1.55 mm² | 1.18 mm² (0.27 to 2.09); P = 0.012 |
  | B: maximum vs operator pulses, at the maximum arc | 5.50 ± 1.19 vs 5.56 ± 1.45 mm² | −0.06 mm² (−0.78 to 0.67); P = 0.873 |

  The authors conclude that RA + IVL increases stent area at the nodule, that maximum pulses do not increase it without a nodule, and that the OCT phenotype guides the choice, pending multicenter validation.
strengths: |-
  - Randomized, with serial OCT in every patient.
  - A question defined by imaging phenotype, as practice already does.
  - Difference, 95% CI and n per arm reported; the numbers check out when recalculated.
problems: |-
  - **Stent and vessel size not separated from the effect of RA.** Mean stent diameter was 3.45 vs 3.27 mm (difference 0.18; 95% CI −0.01 to 0.37; my calculation). The corresponding nominal area differs by about 0.95 mm², close to the 1.18 mm² gain (my calculation). Distal reference area was also larger with RA + IVL (7.26 vs 6.14 mm²; P = 0.105). Without blinding, the operator chose the stent: a larger stent may be an effect of RA or an operator choice. Relative expansion at the nodule did not differ (112.5% vs 105.1%; P = 0.309). Adjusting only for baseline MLA, the difference falls to 0.87 mm² (0.09 to 1.65; my calculation).
  - **Imaging endpoint without a clinical threshold.** The paper defines no minimal relevant MSA difference; 1.18 mm² is 18.9% of the IVL-arm mean (my calculation). There were no clinical events at 30 days (0/120). The RA arm had longer procedures (87.0 vs 76.0 min; P = 0.036) and more air kerma (1.79 vs 1.36 Gy; P = 0.028).
  - **Cohort B lacks precision.** No sample size calculation, 54 patients instead of the 80 planned. The smallest effect detectable with 80% power is about 1.01 mm² (my calculation). The CI is compatible with a loss of up to 0.78 mm² and a gain of up to 0.67 mm²: "no significant difference" describes precision, not equivalence. Unstable angina or NSTEMI (44% vs 15%) and LAD (89% vs 70%) were imbalanced.
  - **Uncorrected secondary endpoints in the main message.** Eccentricity, overall MSA and QCA have P values between 0.001 and 0.05 without adjustment, as declared, and the central illustration highlights a "more circular and symmetric" stent.
  - **Phenotype-based selection without a comparator arm.** RA + IVL was not tested without a nodule, and maximum pulses were not tested with one. The authors state that the cohorts were not designed for direct comparison.
opinion: |-
  In 66 patients with a nodule, at a high-volume center with OCT in all, RA before IVL gave a stent area 1.18 mm² larger at the nodule. Part of the gain may come from larger stents and vessels.

  Not shown: clinical benefit, equivalence between pulse strategies, and a phenotype-based algorithm. No arm tests the same strategy in both phenotypes, so phenotype-based selection remains a hypothesis.

  To me, it is a good starting point for a multicenter trial with clinical endpoints.
